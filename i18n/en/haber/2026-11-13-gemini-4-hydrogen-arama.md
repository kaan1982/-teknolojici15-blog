On 12 November, Google released Hydrogen, the third model in the Gemini 4 family. It joins Carbon and Argon and is positioned by Google as a fast "everyday" model for daily coding, tool use and multimodal work. Deep, long-running reasoning tasks remain with Carbon and Argon.

## What changes in Search?

Hydrogen is now the default model in AI Mode in Google Search, and it is used for harder questions in AI Overviews. Robby Stein, Google's vice president of product for Search, described it as the biggest model update ever to come to Search. According to the company, the model now better understands multi-part, misspelled or very detailed questions and follows instructions more closely.

Search breaks a question into subtopics and runs several queries at the same time; Google says this will surface a wider range of websites. Answers will include more images, comparison tables and small question-specific tools. The update is rolling out to all users worldwide in every language AI Mode supports.

## Numbers for developers

API pricing is 1.50 dollars per million input tokens and 7.50 dollars per million output tokens. The context window is about 1 million tokens. Google says that on default settings tasks finish 2.5 times faster than with Gemini 3.8 Flash; however, most of the shared measurements come from the company's own tests and have not been independently verified. The model is also rolling out gradually in the Gemini app.
