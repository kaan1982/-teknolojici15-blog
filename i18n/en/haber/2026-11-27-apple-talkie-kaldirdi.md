Apple has removed Talkie, the companion app from Chinese AI company MiniMax, from the App Store worldwide. The company cited sexual content in the app as the reason, but did not say whether this related to content shown to children. The decision became known after a joint investigation published on November 25 by the UK based Bureau of Investigative Journalism (TBIJ) and Australian broadcaster ABC News.

## What did the investigation find?

Talkie is an app where users chat by text and voice with AI generated characters, some of them built around romantic relationships and role play. The investigation documented conversations that parents in Australia and the US found on their children's phones. One character first made degrading remarks to a 12 year old girl and then moved on to possessive messages. In the US, a mother found more than 40 conversations involving violence and unwanted romantic advances on the phones of her daughters, aged 10 and 13.

The app was listed with an 18+ age rating on both the App Store and Google Play, but age was set by the user's own statement. According to a survey by Australia's online safety regulator, about 50,000 Australian children aged 10 to 17 said they used Talkie. According to the investigation, MiniMax engineers followed a design that penalized characters that could not keep a conversation going during testing; on Talkie and its Chinese sibling Xingye, users spend more than 70 minutes a day on average.

## What does it mean for MiniMax?

In October, Talkie had about 11 million monthly users across web and app. According to Reuters, Talkie and Xingye made up more than 60 percent of MiniMax's revenue in 2024. At the time the investigation was published, the app could still be downloaded on Google Play.

## Why does it matter?

Unlike assistants that answer questions, companion apps are built to bind users emotionally and keep them for a long time; when age verification is weak, this design hits children hardest. The same week, New York signed a law banning the sale of toys with chatbots for five years. Store moderation and age checks are becoming one of the hardest tests for app platforms in the AI era.
