Anthropic, the developer of the Claude models, has bought roughly $3.4 billion worth of gas-fired generators to supply electricity to its data centers. The Financial Times reported the deal on 8 November, and Bloomberg and CNBC also carried the news. According to the newspaper, it is the largest single purchase of energy equipment by an AI company known to date.

## What is in the deal?

The generators have a combined capacity of 1.7 gigawatts. The seller is OnePWR Solutions, an energy equipment supplier based in San Antonio, Texas. According to the report, Anthropic originally planned only to buy electricity from OnePWR. It later changed course and directly took over heavy-duty generator orders that the company had placed with Caterpillar about two years ago and needed financing to complete. The commitment was made at the end of September; the deal also covers the engineering and construction work needed to install the generators.

Anthropic did not acquire OnePWR, and the company's employees did not move to Anthropic. It has not been disclosed which data centers the generators will be installed at. Neither company responded to requests for comment.

## The energy bottleneck

The computing power needed to train and run AI models is growing fast, and that power demands huge amounts of electricity. Connecting to the grid can take years in many regions, and there are long waiting lists for equipment such as generators and turbines. Anthropic previously relied on outside partners for energy, chips and data centers. The company has said it plans to spend more than $895 billion in the coming years, mostly on computing infrastructure.

## Why it matters

In the AI race, electricity is now a scarce resource, not just chips. A model company buying energy equipment directly instead of waiting in line shows how close the industry has moved to energy infrastructure.
