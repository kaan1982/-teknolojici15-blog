Anthropic, the developer of the Claude models, announced the price range for its IPO on 10 November in an updated prospectus filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC): $70 to $78 per share. The company will trade on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT".

## How much money, at what value?

Anthropic is selling 825 million Class A shares. That means proceeds of between $57.75 billion and $64.35 billion. In addition, $26 billion of non-voting shares will be sold to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC at the IPO price. According to TechCrunch, Google and Nvidia are each taking $10 billion of this package. Together, the two can reach a total of $90.4 billion.

The prospectus does not state a company valuation. Depending on the calculation method, the value comes out between $1.02 trillion and $1.4 trillion. The company raised money in May at a valuation of $965 billion.

## The mistake a day earlier

The draft sent to banks on Monday listed 867 million shares and a range of $70-80. Anthropic said this information was incorrect and on Tuesday published a corrected document with smaller figures.

According to CNBC's sources, the price will be set on 18 November and trading will begin on 19 November. Up to 20 percent of the shares are reserved for retail investors; this share is usually between 5 and 10 percent. Each of the seven co-founders is selling about 3.4 million shares. Through a special share class, the founders will continue to hold 50.1 percent of the votes.

The figures in the filing are striking too: an annualized revenue run rate of $81 billion at the end of October, and a net loss of $104 billion for the first nine months.

## Why it matters

This is a candidate for one of the largest IPOs in tech history, and it comes right after a sharp drop in AI stocks. Demand will be a live test of how much investors still trust AI.
