Google has made a significant change to the privacy rules of its Gemini chat app. According to an update in the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub, new conversations will be kept for "about two months" even when chat history is turned off.

## What changed?

According to Gizmodo, citing an archived page from October, this period used to be 72 hours. In other words, the conversations of a user who turned off history were deleted within three days; the new period is about 20 times longer. Gizmodo points out that the default periods announced by OpenAI and Anthropic are 30 days, and that Google's new period is roughly double that. The updated notice states that stored chats are also reviewed with the help of human reviewers.

Google cites safety as the reason: stored chats will be used to respond to users, process feedback and protect the company, its users and the public.

## The setting that comes in return

The change also has a positive side. According to Android Authority, Google is separating chat history from AI training. You can now keep your history while turning off the use of your chats for model training. There will be one training setting for text and uploads, and a separate one for audio and Gemini Live video and screen sharing. History can be kept for 3, 18 (default) or 36 months, left indefinitely or deleted manually. The update will roll out worldwide in the coming weeks.

## Why does it matter?

Many users treated the "turn off history" button as meaning "forget". After this change, that button leaves conversations on Google's servers for two months. It is worth knowing this difference before typing sensitive information into AI chats.
