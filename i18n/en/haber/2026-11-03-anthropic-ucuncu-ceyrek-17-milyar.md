Anthropic, the developer of the Claude models, submitted an amended S-1 prospectus to the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on Monday, November 2. The company, which filed for an IPO last week, disclosed its third quarter figures for the first time in this document.

## What do the numbers say?

Anthropic's third quarter revenue, covering July, August and September, approached $17 billion. That is an increase of more than $5 billion over the previous quarter. According to Forbes, revenue grew 1,127 percent compared with a year earlier, meaning it rose roughly twelvefold.

The profit side is mixed. After posting an operating profit of $974 million in the second quarter, the company recorded an operating loss of $567 million in the third quarter. Anthropic attributes this loss mainly to $5 billion of compute purchased through short-term deals. Total compute and infrastructure spending for the quarter was $9.9 billion, four times higher than a year earlier. Forbes reports that the net loss for the quarter was $23.3 billion.

The most striking item in the document, however, is the forward-looking commitments. Anthropic has pledged to spend about $895 billion on compute and infrastructure, and roughly half of that is expected to be paid by the end of 2030.

## Where does the IPO stand?

The company has not yet announced the price range, the number of shares to be sold or the size of the offering. That information will come in later amendments to the prospectus.

## Why does it matter?

The business model of AI labs can now be read from an open ledger: demand is very strong, but every new customer means more chips, energy and data centers. Investors will weigh how quickly a commitment burden approaching $900 billion can be covered by revenue growth.
