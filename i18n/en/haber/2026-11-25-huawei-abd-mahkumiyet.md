On November 24, after a three-month trial in federal court for the Eastern District of New York, a jury found Chinese telecom giant Huawei and three of its subsidiaries guilty. According to the US Department of Justice, the verdicts cover bank and wire fraud, conspiracy to defraud the United States, violations of Iran sanctions, conspiracy to commit money laundering and conspiracy to steal trade secrets.

## What came out at trial?

Prosecutors said Huawei ran its business in Iran through a company called "Skycom" and hid this from banks, gaining access to billions of dollars in transactions and US dollar clearing services. According to the department, the company built internet infrastructure for the Iranian regime and supplied security cameras that could be used to monitor protests.

The intellectual property side has some striking details. The court heard that Huawei engineers copied the source code of Cisco routers and tried to hide it by writing Cisco's name backwards as "ocsic". Photos of "Tappy", T-Mobile's robot for testing touchscreens, were sent to China, and one employee carried a part of the robot out of the lab in a backpack.

## What does Huawei say?

The jury acquitted Huawei of the racketeering (RICO) charge. The company welcomed that decision and argued that the remaining charges have no legal or factual basis. According to Huawei, the five trade secret disputes listed by prosecutors were settled years ago as civil cases. The company said it would review the verdicts and take the necessary legal steps.

Sentencing is set for September 14, 2027. Since a company cannot go to prison, what is at stake is fines, forfeiture and restitution. The charges were first announced in January 2019, a month after the company's chief financial officer, Meng Wanzhou, was detained in Canada.

## Why does it matter?

The longest legal front in the US-China tech rivalry has, for the first time, ended in a jury verdict. The size of the penalty and possible appeals could weigh on Huawei's business partnerships in the West for a long time.
