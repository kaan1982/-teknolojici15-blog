This page explains what data is collected when you visit Teknolojici15 and how you can control it.

## Measurement: Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to improve the site. It collects information such as which pages are read, how long you stay, how far you scroll, which links you click, device type, browser, language and approximate location (country, city). Google Analytics 4 does not log IP addresses. This information is not used to identify you personally and is never sold. You can read how Google processes this data on [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Advertising: Google AdSense

Some areas of the site show Google AdSense ads, marked as "Advertisement". Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of a third party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Consent and cookie settings

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform. Measurement and advertising cookies are not used without consent. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the page. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings.
